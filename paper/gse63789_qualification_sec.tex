\subsection{A new yeast source is joinable, but raw counts are not the old normalized endpoint}
A frozen ingest-only unit qualifies GSE63789 before fitting or scoring transfer. Official series and sample text exports identify WT mRNA sample GSM1557442 and WT footprint sample GSM1557447, aligned uniquely to yeast genome R63. RNA reads longer than 26 nucleotides and footprint reads of 28--31 nucleotides are retained by the source processing. The actual WT supplementary header is \texttt{Name, Length, Sum-mRNA, Sum-FP, FP}; the final field contains a positional footprint vector. These are documented counts, not the RPKM fields used in the earlier GSE75897 density-ratio audit. Source: \url{https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE63789}.

All 5,225 rows have distinct gene names and positive RNA and footprint totals. In every row the positional-vector length equals the reported Length, and its integer sum equals Sum-FP. Thus a positional-vector total does not need to be invented or substituted for the source's total count. Exactly 5,142 identifiers join uniquely to the existing R64 reference CDS; 83 do not join, and no joined gene has ambiguous CDS records. Of the joined rows, 5,110 have three times the reported Length equal to the CDS nucleotide length, while 32 differ. The factor-of-three match is consistent with codon-scale geometry, not nucleotide positions; the Length field must not be blindly treated as a nucleotide count. The 32 mismatches also require explicit handling before a positional or length-matched analysis.

There are 4,785 gene identifiers in common with the old 5,068-gene GSE75897 analysis. This is gene reuse in the same organism, not evidence that the measurements are identical or that a cross-source association is independent of gene identity. The new series supplies one WT RNA and one WT footprint sample, not replicate uncertainty. Its source processing does not turn a raw footprint/RNA ratio into measured protein yield, initiation efficiency or the author's model-based synthesis-rate estimator.

Qualification is therefore conditional. A separate frozen scoring unit must define its raw-count endpoint or normalization, cross-source rank versus absolute calibration goal, exclusions for R63/R64 length differences and use of overlapping genes. No prediction, transfer correlation, model refit, tRNA intervention inference or codon-design benefit is reported by this ingest unit. Source hash, full mismatch histogram, unmatched identifiers and row checks are retained in \path{results/gse63789_qualification_audit.json}; plan and runner are \path{scripts/gse63789_qualification_plan.json} and \path{scripts/gse63789_qualification_audit.py}. Raw supplementary records are not redistributed. The content-page gate remains uncertified.
