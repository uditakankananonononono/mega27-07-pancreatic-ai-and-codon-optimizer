\subsection{Pinned PaxDb version geometry exposes a checkpoint identity defect}
The new geometry-qualified PaxDb 6.0 integrated MG1655 source is compared with the exploratory CLI checkpoint's pinned PaxDb 4.2 and GCF\_000005845.2 CDS inputs, not silently substituted for them. Source hashes are verified. The old 3,746 dataset rows represent only 3,737 unique locus tags: eight loci have multiple CDS records, totaling seventeen records. Reconstructing the original seed-zero row split finds locus b2592 in both training and test sets. The earlier 0.564 checkpoint correlation is therefore not certified as a strictly locus-disjoint holdout; the historical score is retained without pretending this audit retrained it.

For the comparison, all eight ambiguous old loci are quarantined, as are the new source's twenty-nine protein-sequence mismatches. Of 4,065 exact-protein-matched new rows, 3,708 overlap an unambiguous old input locus: 3,148 were training loci and 560 test loci. All 3,708 have identical full CDS DNA and identical encoded first-1,500-nt prefixes across these reference snapshots. The remaining 357 new eligible loci are outside the unambiguous old input set, not automatically independent biological tests. The old dataset has 568 CDS records longer than 1,500 nt; the new exact-protein subset has 561. This documents truncation geometry, not its effect on prediction.

No model is fitted or scored here, and integrated abundance versions can share source studies and genes. MG1655 reference identity does not establish the NCM3722 experimental strain sequence or paired Ribo/RNA measurements. Correct future scoring needs an explicit sequence/locus eligibility rule and a locus-grouped split, not a gene-name join or a row-wise split through alternate CDS records. Exact hashes, exclusions and counts are in \path{results/paxdb_v6_geometry_audit.json}. Sources: \url{https://pax-db.org/downloads/6.0/datasets/paxdb-abundance-files-v6.0/511145/511145-WHOLE_ORGANISM-integrated.txt} and NC\_000913.3. PaxDb's CC BY 4.0 terms require attribution and changes to be stated, with the original-submitter IP caveat retained; this audit compares identity geometry rather than redistributing the full source.

\subsection{A separately frozen unique-locus checkpoint rerun}
A repair experiment excludes all seventeen records belonging to the eight repeated loci, without choosing a longest or first CDS. This leaves 3,729 unique-locus rows on the same admitted PaxDb 4.2 source. The seed-zero 85\% split now has 3,169 training loci and 560 test loci, with explicit IDs confirming no locus intersection. The architecture, first-1,500-nt encoding, five epochs, batch size and Adam settings remain those of the earlier exploratory checkpoint. Label normalization uses training rows only. A separate checkpoint is stored; the historical model is not overwritten.

Held-out Spearman is 0.594738, compared with the archived 0.564187 row-split checkpoint. This difference is not an estimate of leakage's causal effect: exclusions and the permutation over the changed dataset also change training and test membership. A cleaner split need not lower a single observed score. This rerun repairs exact locus overlap, not related-sequence leakage, independent source/condition validation or evidence that optimized synonymous sequences increase protein yield. The freeze and explicit split/prediction ledger are \path{scripts/cli_unique_locus_plan.json} and \path{results/cli_unique_locus_audit.json}. No PaxDb 6.0 outcome is scored as independent validation.
