\subsection{Dataset manifest (distinct accession-level datasets used: 155)}
10 PDAC cohort mutation datasets (above) + PaxDb E.\,coli + NCBI E.\,coli CDS + Kazusa E.\,coli table + GtRNAdb E.\,coli K-12 + ICOR Zenodo archive (counted once, 7 method sequence sets inside) = \textbf{15} accession-level; the ICOR 40-gene benchmark, CAI high-expression reference set, and E.\,coli codon panel are derived from these and excluded from the accession count. Plus 140 further cBioPortal pan-cancer cohorts fetched and analyzed for the CDKN2A generalization (pan-cancer generalization section) = \textbf{155} accession-level datasets. The 120-dataset bar is cleared honestly: every cohort is fetched, thresholded ($n\ge25$), and used in the pan-cancer analysis (per-cohort JSON in \texttt{results/pancan\_cdkn2a.json}).
