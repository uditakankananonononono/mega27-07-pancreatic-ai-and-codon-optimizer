\subsection{A not-admitted second-source eligibility audit, without ratios or validation}
The publisher's licensed PLOS1010641 supplementary RNA and footprint RPKM workbooks provide a candidate measurement source, not a strict-admitted validation set. A frozen byte-verified eligibility audit retains all 4,321 rows and 36 nominally matching endpoint columns. Gene name plus CDS start/end gives 4,321 unique keys; name alone has 46 extra duplicate rows. These coordinate keys are not yet verified sequence/strand identities. Matching condition/replicate labels do not establish same biological aliquots, and different BioSample IDs leave that pairing gate unresolved.

The RNA workbook has 15,533 zero cells and the footprint workbook 4,059. A prospective no-pseudocount log-ratio rule would require both modalities strictly positive. Under nominal column correspondence, eligible row counts range from 3,024 to 4,118, and only 2,323 rows are positive in both modalities across all 36 columns. These are eligibility counts, not computed ratios, selected expression cutoffs, or inferred absence of gene expression. No condition is removed, no model is fitted or scored, and no validation is claimed.

Reference CDS strand/sequences, run mapping and cross-source predictor geometry remain unresolved. In particular, this E.~coli candidate cannot be scored by silently transferring the yeast model. The reported units are RPKM; even after admission, a footprint/RNA ratio would describe relative translational occupancy rather than absolute protein output or synonymous-design benefit. Exact per-column counts, original file hashes and the frozen rule are in \path{results/gse182100_eligibility_audit.json}.
